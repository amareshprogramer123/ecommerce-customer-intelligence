# 🛒 E-Commerce Customer Intelligence & Sales Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

The **E-Commerce Customer Intelligence & Sales Analytics** project is an advanced MySQL-based data analytics project designed to analyze customer behavior, sales performance, product performance, profitability, and business growth.

The project simulates a real-world e-commerce business environment and uses SQL to transform transactional data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

This project focuses on answering important business questions such as:

* Who are the highest-value customers?
* Which products and categories generate the most revenue?
* Which products generate the highest profit?
* How is revenue changing month by month?
* Which customers are repeat buyers?
* Which customers may require retention attention?
* Which cities and states generate the most revenue?
* What is the average order value?
* Which products have high revenue but low profit?
* Which payment methods are most frequently used?
* Which products have never been purchased?
* How can customers be segmented based on their purchasing behavior?

## 🗄️ Database Structure

The database contains **7 relational tables**:

| Table                | Description                              |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| `customers`          | Customer information                     |
| `orders`             | Customer order information               |
| `order_items`        | Products included in each order          |
| `products`           | Product details, prices, costs and stock |
| `categories`         | Product category information             |
| `payments`           | Payment transaction information          |
| `customer_addresses` | Customer location information            |

### 🔗 Database Relationship

```text
Customers
    │
    ├── Orders
    │      │
    │      └── Order_Items ─── Products ─── Categories
    │
    └── Customer_Addresses

Orders ─── Payments
```

## 📈 Example Business Questions

### 1. Top Customers

Identify customers who generate the highest total revenue.

### 2. Top Products

Find the best-performing products based on sales revenue.

### 3. Category Performance

Analyze which product categories contribute the most revenue.

### 4. Monthly Revenue Growth

Use window functions such as `LAG()` to compare current-month revenue with the previous month.

### 5. Customer Lifetime Value

Calculate the total revenue generated by each customer throughout their purchasing history.

### 6. Customer Segmentation

Classify customers based on their purchasing behavior, such as:

* High-value customers
* Regular customers
* Low-value customers

### 7. Product Profitability

Compare product revenue and cost to identify highly profitable and low-profit products.

### 8. RFM-Style Analysis

Analyze customers using:

* **Recency** – How recently they purchased
* **Frequency** – How frequently they purchased
* **Monetary Value** – How much they spent
